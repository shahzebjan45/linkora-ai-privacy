Linkora AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Linkora AI ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Linkora AI collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application and connect your LinkedIn account.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Linkora AI may process:

  • Your name and basic profile information.
  • Your email address when provided by an authentication provider.
  • LinkedIn account information made available through authorized LinkedIn APIs.
  • LinkedIn authorization tokens required to provide connected features.
  • Posts, drafts, media, and content that you create or save in Linkora AI.
  • Application usage and technical information required to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate Linkora AI features.
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Allow users to create, save, edit, schedule, and manage content.
  • Provide authorized LinkedIn-related functionality.
  • Generate AI-assisted content when requested by the user.
  • Improve reliability, security, and performance of the application.

3. LinkedIn Data

If you connect your LinkedIn account, Linkora AI accesses only the LinkedIn information and permissions authorized by you and made available through LinkedIn's APIs.

LinkedIn data is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user. We do not sell LinkedIn user data or use LinkedIn data for unrelated purposes.

4. AI Processing

When you request AI-assisted content generation, information necessary to generate the requested content may be processed by our configured AI service provider. We use this processing to provide the requested application functionality.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Authentication credentials, authorization tokens, and other sensitive information are intended to be handled using appropriate security mechanisms.

6. Data Sharing

We may use trusted service providers that help us operate Linkora AI, such as hosting, database, media storage, authentication, and AI infrastructure providers.

We do not sell personal information or LinkedIn user data.

7. LinkedIn Publishing

When you explicitly use a LinkedIn publishing feature, Linkora AI may send the content you request to LinkedIn through authorized LinkedIn APIs. Publishing actions are performed according to the permissions granted by you.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, or as otherwise requested by the user.

9. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Linkora AI account and associated application data by contacting us through the contact information provided with the application.

Disconnecting your LinkedIn account may also stop future access to LinkedIn data through the application's authorization.

10. Third-Party Services

Linkora AI may integrate with third-party services, including LinkedIn and infrastructure or AI providers. Their handling of information may also be governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

11. Children's Privacy

Linkora AI is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning Linkora AI, please contact the Linkora AI development team.
